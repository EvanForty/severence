    Coverage is not insurance. Payouts are never guaranteed. Each one is capped by the coin’s actual reserve balance and fixed protocol rules, and the reserve can only pay what trading fees have put into it.

    Severance coverage.

    The longer a wallet holds, the more of a losing exit the reserve can cover. The clock starts on your first buy and buying more never resets it.

    Coverage ladder

    Maximum share of a realized loss the reserve can cover.

    Under 1 hour held, no coverage. Selling part of a bag keeps the clock on the rest.

    How the payout is worked out

    Four inputs, checked at the moment you sell.

    Average costTracked per wallet across every buy, so your loss is measured against what you actually paid.
    Exit valueWhat your tokens sell for. A sell at or above your cost gets no coverage.
    EligibilityYour tier on the ladder, plus anything you compounded into your protection balance.
    Reserve balanceThe live balance. If it can’t cover the full amount, you get what it can pay.
